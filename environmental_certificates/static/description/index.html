Complete Supplier Certificate Tracking

Bring compliance under control with intelligent automation

📋
Centralized Dashboard
Access all supplier certificates from a dedicated Environmental Certificates tab on every vendor record. View certificate types, issue dates, expiry dates, and attached files at a glance.
⚙️
Flexible Configuration
Configure certificate categories and types directly in Settings. Add, edit, or remove certificate types without code changes to match evolving industry standards.
🔄
Automated Lifecycle Tracking
Daily automatic checks classify certificates with color-coded status indicators, making compliance management effortless.

Smart Status Classification:

✅ Active More than 30 days before expiration

⚠️ Expiring Less than 30 days remaining

❌ Expired Past the expiration date

Color indicators (green/yellow/red) make status visible at a glance, allowing your team to prioritize follow-up actions and maintain continuous compliance.

Powerful Automation Features

Reduce manual work and never miss an expiration

📧 Automated Notifications

  • Email reminders to suppliers before expiration, on expiration, or if no certificate is uploaded
  • Configurable notification frequency (7/14/30 days)
  • Email alerts to administrators when suppliers upload or update certificates
  • Full logging of all notifications in Odoo Chatter for complete audit trails

🌐 Supplier Portal Integration

Empower your suppliers to manage their own compliance documentation through secure Customer Portal access. Suppliers can:

  • Log in securely to upload and update certificates
  • View current certificate statuses
  • Attach supporting documents
  • Manage everything without internal Odoo database access

Smart Filters & Reporting

Real-time insights for proactive management

Quickly filter suppliers by expired certificate status to prioritize follow-up actions. Generate real-time reports to maintain compliance and identify risks before they become issues.

All activities are logged in Odoo Chatter, providing complete transparency and audit trails for compliance reporting and internal reviews.

🔐 Role-Based Access Control

  • Administrator – Full control: add, edit, delete, and configure certificate types
  • Purchasing Manager – Add and edit certificates for supplier management
  • Internal Users – View-only access for team visibility
  • Supplier Portal User – Add and update their own certificates securely
